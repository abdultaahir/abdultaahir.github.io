FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you use AI to write the emails?＋

Tools can support research and workflow, but the final messaging is shaped, written and edited around the actual buyer, offer and context.

How fast can a campaign launch?＋

Timing depends on scope and research depth. A focused cold-email project can move quickly once the offer, audience and inputs are clear.

Can you audit an existing campaign without rewriting everything?＋

Yes. I can review the current sequence, identify the biggest messaging problems and recommend what should be kept, cut or rewritten.

Do you handle sending and deliverability?＋

My core responsibility is strategy and copy. I can work alongside your existing sending setup or team, but I do not present copy alone as a guarantee of deliverability.
