Three gaps worth fixing.

01 / Headline reads like a translation

Current wording was unnatural for English readers. Suggested: “Turn your week into LinkedIn posts that bring you clients.”

02 / Proof doesn't match the promise

Ease-of-use testimonials do not demonstrate the promised client outcomes. Put genuinely relevant evidence under the headline if it exists.

03 / Signup ends at a paywall

The audit observed pricing after sign-up but no obvious free trial. Follow-up emails could explain the product's “aha” moment and bring readers back.
