OUTREACH · EDITORIAL

Why cold emails get ignored.

Most inboxes don't suffer from a shortage of messages. They suffer from a shortage of reasons to care.

When an opening could go to fifty unrelated companies without changing a word, it isn't personalisation. It's a mail merge.

The useful questions are simple: Why this person? Why this moment? And why is your request easy to answer?

Start with a real observation. Connect it to one relevant problem. Then ask for one small next step. A good email doesn't need five paragraphs to make its point.
